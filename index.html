Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2025  ·  Dallas, Texas

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spotly to pay for parking, we collect the following information:

  • License plate number — to validate and record your parking session.
  • Phone number — to send you a text receipt confirming your payment.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never store your card number, CVV, or full payment details on our servers.
  • Session data — including lot location, session start time, duration, and amount paid.
  • Device and usage data — such as browser type and IP address, collected automatically when you visit our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm your parking payment.
  • Send you a text receipt via SMS.
  • Provide proof of payment if requested by parking enforcement.
  • Share authorized session data with the property owner of the lot you parked in, for enforcement purposes only.
  • Improve our service, troubleshoot issues, and detect fraud.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Text Messages (SMS)

By providing your phone number, you consent to receiving a one-time SMS receipt from Spotly after each payment. Standard messaging rates from your carrier may apply. You will not be enrolled in any ongoing SMS marketing program. To opt out, simply do not provide your phone number at checkout.

4. Sharing of Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Stripe — our payment processor, to complete your transaction. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Property owners — the owner of the lot you parked in receives your license plate and session time for enforcement purposes. They do not receive your phone number or payment details.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of Spotly or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain your session data (plate, lot, time, amount) for up to 90 days to support dispute resolution and enforcement. After 90 days, session records are deleted from our active database. Phone numbers are not retained after your receipt is delivered.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at yourspotly@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including HTTPS encryption and Stripe's PCI-compliant payment infrastructure. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Spotly is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Spotly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at yourspotly@gmail.com. We are based in Dallas, Texas.
